1. Getting Started

CRM Pro is a web application — there is nothing to install. Open the app URL in your browser and sign in with the account your administrator created for you.

Signing in

  • Company single sign-on: click Sign in and use your work email/password, or a connected provider (Google/Facebook) if your organization enabled it.
  • Email & password: some organizations use a direct email/password login instead of single sign-on. Use Forgot password on the login screen if you need a reset link.
  • First-time staff: you'll receive an email invitation from an administrator with a link to set your password before your first login.

Finding your way around

Once signed in you land on the Dashboard. The left sidebar lists every module you have access to (Leads, Opportunities, Accounts, Contacts, Activities, Tickets, Reports, Automations, Users, Settings). Menu items you can't use are hidden — what you see is based on your role.

If a module you expect to see is missing, ask your administrator to check your role and permissions (see Users, Roles & Permissions).

2. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your daily starting point. It shows a real-time snapshot of the business: open pipeline value, new leads, tasks due today/overdue, ticket volume, and team performance — scoped to what your role is allowed to see (your own records, your team's, or the whole organization).

Charts are click-through: selecting a pipeline stage or a lead-source slice jumps you to the filtered list behind it.

3. Leads

A Lead is a potential customer who hasn't been qualified yet — an inbound web enquiry, a trade-show contact, a cold-call prospect. Leads keep the pipeline free of half-formed records until they're worth turning into a real Account/Contact/Opportunity.

Capturing leads

  • Manually: click New Lead and fill in name, company, contact details and source.
  • Public web form: the /capture page is a public, no-login form you can embed on your website or share as a link — submissions land directly in the Leads list.

Scoring & qualification

Leads are automatically scored based on fields like company size, engagement, and source quality, helping reps prioritize hot prospects over tyre-kickers. Move a lead through statuses (New → Contacted → Qualified → Converted/Disqualified) as work progresses.

Converting a lead

Qualifying a lead and clicking Convert creates the real records in one step: an Account (if a company doesn't already exist), a Contact, and an Opportunity — carrying over the lead's details so nothing has to be re-typed.

RoleCan do
Sales RepCreate, view and work their own assigned leads

AdminFull access, including lead-routing and scoring configuration

4. Opportunities & Pipeline

An Opportunity is a deal in progress with a value, an expected close date, and a stage. The Pipeline view is a Kanban board — drag a deal card between stages (e.g. Qualifying → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won/Lost) as it advances.

Each Opportunity links back to its Account and Contact, so anyone opening the company record can see every deal ever run against it, won or lost.

Marking a deal Closed Lost requires a reason — this feeds the loss analysis in Reports.

5. Accounts & Contacts

Accounts are companies; Contacts are the individual people at those companies. This is the central address book every other module (deals, tickets, activities) hangs off.

Accounts

An Account profile holds company details (industry, size, website), every Contact at that company, every Opportunity ever opened with them, and the full interaction history.

Contacts

A Contact record is a person — not necessarily a portal user. You can store a procurement manager's or an assistant's details without granting them a login. Only Customers (see Service & Support) get portal access.

Communication preferences (e.g. "email only, no calls") are recorded on the Contact and respected by Activities and Automation.

6. Activities, Tasks & Calendar

Activities are the "what happens next" layer: tasks, calls, meetings and logged communications tied to a Lead, Account, Contact, or Opportunity.

Tasks

Create a task with a due date and owner; it shows up on that person's Dashboard and in the team's workload view so nothing is dropped when someone is out.

Communication log

Every call, email, or meeting note logged against a record appears on a unified timeline — open any Account/Contact/Lead and scroll through its entire history in one place.

Calendar

Export tasks and meetings as an .ics file to subscribe from Outlook, Google Calendar, or Apple Calendar.

7. Automation & Workflows

Automations turn manual habits into rules the system enforces automatically. An administrator configures trigger-based actions such as:

  • "When a lead is qualified → create a follow-up task and send a welcome email."
  • "When a record sits untouched for N days → escalate it to the manager (SLA tracking)."
  • "When a new web lead arrives → notify the assigned rep instantly."

This closes the gap between process (what should always happen) and practice (what a busy rep remembers to do).

8. Tickets & Knowledge Base

The helpdesk module keeps the relationship going after a deal closes.

Tickets

Customers (or staff on their behalf) raise support tickets. Staff work them through a status flow with response-time tracking, so "we answer within a day" is a measured SLA, not a hope. Customers with portal accounts see only their own organization's tickets; staff see the full helpdesk queue.

Knowledge Base

Staff author help articles in the KB editor. Published, public articles appear on the Help Center (/faq) — a no-login page anyone can search, with helpful/not-helpful feedback on each article, deflecting repeat questions before they become tickets.

9. Reports & Analytics

Two ways to see the numbers:

  • Dashboards — a ready-made, real-time overview of pipeline, conversion rate, revenue and team performance. No setup required.
  • Custom Report Builder — a point-and-click tool for specific questions the dashboards don't answer, e.g. "All lost opportunities in Q3 where value > $10,000." Save a report and re-run it any time.

Reporting never shows a person more than they can already see elsewhere in the CRM — the same row-level visibility rules apply (see Teams & Territories).

10. Teams & Territories

Teams group users (e.g. "East Region Sales") and determine who can see whose records. An administrator assigns a team to a manager and members; the visibility matrix then controls whether a person sees only their own records, their team's, or every record in the organization.

Records (leads, accounts, opportunities) can be assigned to a specific team or territory so work is routed to the right group automatically.

11. Users, Roles & Permissions

Administrators manage the staff directory under Users. Standard roles:

RoleScope
User / Sales RepEveryday CRM user — sees their own and team-assigned records
ManagerTeam-wide visibility and reassignment rights
Admin / AdministratorFull organization access, configuration, user management
CustomerPortal-only access — their own tickets and KB articles
Platform AdminCross-organization operator role (SaaS deployments only)

Custom roles with fine-grained permissions can also be defined for organizations that need more precise control than the standard roles above.

Adding teammates

Use Add Staff on the Users page to invite a new teammate by email — they receive a link to set their password and sign in.

12. Settings, Billing & Licensing

CRM Pro ships in two modes from a single codebase:

  • Standalone — one company, one installation, no subscriptions or billing screens.
  • SaaS — the CRM operator hosts multiple customer organizations, each on its own subscription plan with self-service billing.

In SaaS mode, an organization's Admin manages their subscription plan and payment details under Settings → Billing. In Standalone mode this section is hidden entirely.

13. Help Center & FAQ

The public Help Center at /faq requires no login. Search or browse by category, read an article, and mark it helpful or not — useful for sending to customers directly, or for staff who want a quick refresher without opening a ticket.
